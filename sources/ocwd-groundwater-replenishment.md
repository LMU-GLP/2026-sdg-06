# Groundwater Replenishment System (GWRS) — Orange County Water District

- **Source:** Orange County Water District (OCWD)
- **URL:** https://www.ocwd.com/gwrs/the-process/ (capacity figures); https://www.ocwd.com/ (cumulative production figure)
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- Current capacity: **130 million gallons per day (MGD)** of purified recycled water.
- Distribution of that capacity:
  - **30 MGD** to seawater intrusion barrier injection wells (Huntington Beach & Fountain Valley).
  - **10 MGD** to Mid-Basin injection wells (Santa Ana).
  - **90 MGD** to percolation basins (Anaheim), recharging the groundwater basin.
- Cumulative production: **506.88 billion gallons produced** (through end of June 2026, per the OCWD homepage counter).
- Purification is a three-step process: **microfiltration, reverse osmosis, and ultraviolet light treatment** (with advanced oxidation), applied to treated wastewater before it is injected or percolated back into the groundwater basin.
- GWRS is described by OCWD as **"a joint collaboration between OCWD and OC San"** (Orange County Sanitation District).

## What the source does not say

The pages read did not give: an acre-feet-per-year conversion, the number of people served, a historical start date, or cost figures. OCWD points readers to a separate technical brochure and its 2024 annual report for those.

## Direct quotes

> "NEW WATER YOU CAN COUNT ON" — OCWD's framing of GWRS on its homepage.

## Summary

GWRS takes treated wastewater that would otherwise be discharged and, through microfiltration, reverse osmosis, and UV treatment, turns it into 130 million gallons a day of purified water that is injected or percolated back into Orange County's groundwater basin — an operating, decades-old example of turning wastewater into a usable water source rather than a discard. It is a working precedent for "use water more wisely" at regional infrastructure scale, distinct from LADWP's stormwater-capture approach already in this wiki.
