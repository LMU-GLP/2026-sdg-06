# South Africa: total household count (2025)

- **Source:** Statista Research Department, citing South African household count data
- **URL:** https://www.statista.com/statistics/1112732/number-of-households-of-south-africa/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **"Approximately 20.1 million"** households in South Africa in 2025 — "roughly half a million more than in the previous year."

## Summary

South Africa had approximately 20.1 million households in 2025. This figure exists in the wiki only to let us convert the 2025 General Household Survey's percentage-based water-interruption figures (see [South Africa water access](./south-africa-water-access.md)) into an approximate headcount — it is a separate source and should not be read as coming from the same survey.
