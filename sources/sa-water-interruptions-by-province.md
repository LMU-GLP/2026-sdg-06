# Water interruptions vary sharply by province and metro — 2025 GHS

- **Source:** BusinessTech, reporting on Statistics South Africa's 2025 General Household Survey
- **URL:** https://businesstech.co.za/news/government/863772/situation-critical-for-south-africa-2/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **Weekly water interruptions by province:** Mpumalanga **36.0%**, Limpopo **25.9%**, KwaZulu-Natal **24.2%**, Western Cape **0.3%**.
- **Households with no interruptions at all:** Western Cape **74.8%**, Free State **47.0%**, Eastern Cape **30.6%**, Mpumalanga **30.0%**, Northern Cape **29.4%**, North West **28.6%**.
- Metro trend: **"in 2015, 7% of households in the metros experienced water interruptions, whereas now, the number has risen to over 30%."**
- The **City of Johannesburg and City of Cape Town** residents were identified as **most likely to experience prolonged outages** among the metros.
- National figures (matches [South Africa water access](./south-africa-water-access.md)): **56.8%** of households experienced water interruptions in 2025; **43.2%** reported none; **37.6%** experienced interruptions lasting at least 2 days.

## Summary

The national 37.6% multi-day-interruption figure hides enormous provincial variance: Mpumalanga's weekly interruption rate (36.0%) is roughly 120 times Western Cape's (0.3%). The interruption problem is not evenly spread — but the survey also names Cape Town specifically, not just poorer inland provinces, among the metros most likely to face prolonged outages in 2025.
