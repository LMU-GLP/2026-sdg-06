# Clean Water Access — Our World in Data

- **Source:** Our World in Data, "Clean Water and Sanitation" (aggregating WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme data)
- **URL:** https://ourworldindata.org/water-access
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **"Almost three-quarters of the world's population uses a safely managed water source. One in four people does not use a safe drinking water source."**
- In the lowest-income countries, **fewer than one-third of the population** accesses safely managed water, and most of that shortfall is in Sub-Saharan Africa.
- **Around 70% of the global population had safe drinking water in 2015**, the SDG baseline year, with only slow increases since.
- **Unsafe water is responsible for more than one million deaths each year globally.**
- High-income countries in Europe have water-related death rates **below 0.1 per 100,000 people**, while Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia often exceed **50 per 100,000** — a **"greater than 1000-fold difference."**
- Urban areas have higher improved water access than rural populations in nearly all countries measured.

## Summary

Roughly a quarter of the world still lacks a safe drinking water source, and the gap is starkly regional: death rates from unsafe water differ by more than a thousandfold between high-income Europe and Sub-Saharan Africa/Asia. This is a global dataset, not a Los Angeles or South Korea figure, and it should not be read as describing either region specifically.
