# South Africa province populations — 2022 census

- **Source:** Wikipedia, "List of South African provinces by population density," citing 2022 South African census figures
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_South_African_provinces_by_population_density
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **Gauteng: 15,099,422** (census, 2022)
- **Limpopo: 6,572,720** (census, 2022)
- **Mpumalanga: 5,143,324** (census, 2022)
- **Free State: 2,964,412** (census, 2022)

## Summary

The four provinces flagged elsewhere in this wiki as having *declining* piped-water access since 2002 (Limpopo, Mpumalanga, Free State, Gauteng) together held approximately 29.8 million people combined, per the 2022 census. This is a **population** count, not a **household** count, so it cannot be combined with the household-based figures elsewhere in this wiki (like the 37.6% interruption rate) without assuming an average household size — a figure not stated in any source in this wiki, so that conversion is not made here.
