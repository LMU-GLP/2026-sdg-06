# SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation — Official Targets

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal6
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **2.1 billion people lacked safely managed drinking water.**
- **3.4 billion people lacked safely managed sanitation services.**
- **1.7 billion people lacked basic hygiene services.**
- **56% of global household wastewater was safely treated.**

## The eight targets, as stated

> **6.1** By 2030, achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all

> **6.2** By 2030, achieve access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene for all and end open defecation

> **6.3** By 2030, improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals

> **6.4** By 2030, substantially increase water-use efficiency across all sectors and ensure sustainable withdrawals

> **6.5** By 2030, implement integrated water resources management at all levels, including transboundary cooperation

> **6.6** By 2020, protect and restore water-related ecosystems, including mountains, forests, wetlands, rivers, aquifers

> **6.a** By 2030, expand international cooperation and capacity-building support to developing countries in water- and sanitation

> **6.b** Support and strengthen the participation of local communities in improving water and sanitation management

## Summary

SDG 6 sets eight targets covering both access (safe drinking water, sanitation) and stewardship (water quality, efficiency, ecosystems, cooperation) — this team's challenge sits mainly under 6.3, 6.4, and 6.b. Target 6.6 was due by 2020 and has already passed, which is itself worth noting when judging progress.
