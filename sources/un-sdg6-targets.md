# SDG Goal 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation: Targets and Indicators

- **Source:** United Nations, Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal6
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures (targets, quoted exactly)

- **Target 6.1:** "By 2030, achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all." (Indicator 6.1.1: proportion of population using safely managed drinking water services.)
- **Target 6.2:** "By 2030, achieve access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene for all and end open defecation." (Indicator 6.2.1: proportion of population using safely managed sanitation services and hand-washing facilities with soap and water.)
- **Target 6.3:** "By 2030, improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals." (Indicators 6.3.1: proportion of wastewater safely treated; 6.3.2: proportion of bodies of water with good ambient water quality.)
- **Target 6.4:** "By 2030, substantially increase water-use efficiency across all sectors and ensure sustainable withdrawals and supply." (Indicators 6.4.1: change in water-use efficiency over time; 6.4.2: level of water stress — freshwater withdrawal as a proportion of available freshwater resources.)
- **Target 6.5:** "By 2030, implement integrated water resources management at all levels, including through transboundary cooperation." (Indicators 6.5.1: degree of integrated water resources management; 6.5.2: proportion of transboundary basin area with operational water cooperation arrangements.)
- **Target 6.6:** "By 2020, protect and restore water-related ecosystems, including mountains, forests, wetlands, rivers, aquifers and lakes." (Indicator 6.6.1: change in extent of water-related ecosystems over time.)
- **Target 6.a:** "By 2030, expand international cooperation and capacity-building support to developing countries in water and sanitation."
- **Target 6.b:** "Support and strengthen participation of local communities in improving water and sanitation management."

## Additional figures cited on the page

- **2.1 billion people** lack safely managed drinking water.
- **3.4 billion people** lack safely managed sanitation.
- **1.7 billion people** lack basic hygiene services.
- **56%** of household wastewater is safely treated globally.
- Water-use efficiency improved from **$17.4/m³ to $21.5/m³** (a 24% increase, per the page's own framing).
- **54% of countries** face water efficiency challenges (below $20/m³).
- Northern Africa and Central/Southern Asia experience extreme water scarcity.

## Summary

SDG 6 sets eight targets covering drinking water access, sanitation, water quality, water-use efficiency, integrated water management, ecosystem protection, and international/community cooperation, each due mostly by 2030 (ecosystems by 2020). The UN's own tracking shows billions still lacking safely managed water, sanitation, or hygiene, meaning the goal is off track by its own indicators.
