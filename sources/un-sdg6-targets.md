# SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation: Official Targets

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal6
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **2.1 billion people lack safely managed drinking water.**
- **3.4 billion people lack safely managed sanitation.**
- **1.7 billion people lack basic hygiene services.**
- **56% of global household wastewater flows were safely treated.**
- **54% of countries face water-use efficiency challenges below $20/m³.**

## The eight targets, quoted exactly

- **Target 6.1:** "By 2030, achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all." *(Indicator 6.1.1: Proportion of population using safely managed drinking water services)*
- **Target 6.2:** "By 2030, achieve access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene for all and end open defecation, paying special attention to the needs of women and girls and those in vulnerable situations." *(Indicator 6.2.1: Proportion of population using safely managed sanitation services and hand-washing facilities with soap and water)*
- **Target 6.3:** "By 2030, improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals and materials, halving the proportion of untreated wastewater and substantially increasing recycling and safe reuse globally." *(Indicators 6.3.1: Proportion of domestic and industrial wastewater flows safely treated; 6.3.2: Proportion of bodies of water with good ambient water quality)*
- **Target 6.4:** "By 2030, substantially increase water-use efficiency across all sectors and ensure sustainable withdrawals and supply of freshwater to address water scarcity and substantially reduce the number of people suffering from water scarcity." *(Indicators 6.4.1: Change in water-use efficiency over time; 6.4.2: Level of water stress: freshwater withdrawal as a proportion of available freshwater resources)*
- **Target 6.5:** "By 2030, implement integrated water resources management at all levels, including through transboundary cooperation as appropriate." *(Indicators 6.5.1: Degree of integrated water resources management; 6.5.2: Proportion of transboundary basin area with an operational arrangement for water cooperation)*
- **Target 6.6:** "By 2020, protect and restore water-related ecosystems, including mountains, forests, wetlands, rivers, aquifers and lakes." *(Indicator 6.6.1: Change in the extent of water-related ecosystems over time)*
- **Target 6.a:** "By 2030, expand international cooperation and capacity-building support to developing countries in water- and sanitation-related activities and programmes, including water harvesting, desalination, water efficiency, wastewater treatment, recycling and reuse technologies." *(Indicator 6.a.1: Amount of water- and sanitation-related official development assistance that is part of a government-coordinated spending plan)*
- **Target 6.b:** "Support and strengthen the participation of local communities in improving water and sanitation management." *(Indicator 6.b.1: Proportion of local administrative units with established and operational policies and procedures for participation of local communities in water and sanitation management)*

## Summary

The UN's SDG 6 sets eight targets covering drinking water, sanitation, water quality, water-use efficiency, ecosystem protection, and community participation, all originally due by 2030 (2020 for ecosystem restoration, target 6.6). By the UN's own count, billions of people still lack safely managed drinking water (2.1 billion), sanitation (3.4 billion), or basic hygiene (1.7 billion), which is the baseline every other source in this wiki has to be read against.
