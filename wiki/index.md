# SDG 6 Wiki: Clean Water and Sanitation — Index

Challenge: **How might we help communities protect clean water and use it more wisely?**

Region not yet chosen — Los Angeles and South Korea both have sources below. Read [la-vs-south-korea.md](la-vs-south-korea.md) first if you're trying to decide.

## Pages

- **[global-water-crisis.md](global-water-crisis.md)** — how big the water access problem is worldwide, and where SDG 6 sets the targets.
- **[los-angeles.md](los-angeles.md)** — LA's problem is drought and a drainage system built to dump rainwater, not keep it; stormwater capture is the buildable fix.
- **[south-korea.md](south-korea.md)** — Seoul's tap water beats WHO standards, but a quarter of residents still won't drink it; the gap is old pipes and inherited distrust, not water quality.
- **[la-vs-south-korea.md](la-vs-south-korea.md)** — side-by-side comparison; flags where the two regions' figures aren't measuring the same thing.
- **[existing-solutions.md](existing-solutions.md)** — programs and businesses already working on this problem, as a reference point for a team solution.
