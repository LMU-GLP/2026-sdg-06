# Wiki Index — SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation

Challenge: **How might we help communities protect clean water and use it more wisely?**

Start here, then branch out to whichever region or angle looks most promising.

## Overview

- [SDG 6 Overview](sdg6-overview.md) — the UN's eight targets for clean water and sanitation, and where global progress stands.
- [Global Water Access: The Numbers](global-water-stats.md) — WHO's dataset on drinking water access, health cost of unsafe water, and water stress.

## Regions

- [Los Angeles: Stormwater Capture](los-angeles-stormwater.md) — LA's drains were built to dump rainwater, not save it; 27+ billion gallons/year is captured, the rest goes to the ocean.
- [Seoul: Tap Water Trust](seoul-tap-water-trust.md) — Seoul's tap water beats WHO standards, but a quarter of residents still won't drink it, mostly over old pipes and legacy distrust.
- [Orange County: Groundwater Replenishment](water-reuse-orange-county.md) — a working program (GWRS) that purifies wastewater and returns 130 million gallons/day to the groundwater basin.

## Comparison

- [Los Angeles vs. South Korea](los-angeles-vs-south-korea.md) — the two regions have different problem types (capture vs. trust), measured in incompatible units; read before picking a region.
