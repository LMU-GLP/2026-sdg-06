# The global water crisis, and what SDG 6 targets

## Scale

- **2.1 billion people lacked safely managed drinking water**, **3.4 billion people lacked safely managed sanitation services**, and **1.7 billion people lacked basic hygiene services** ([UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md)). Only **56% of global household wastewater was safely treated**.
- **One in four people globally lack access to safe drinking water**, and **nearly three-quarters (~73%) of the world's population uses a safely managed water source** ([Our World in Data](../sources/owid-water-access-data.md)).
- **Nearly 1 in 10 people worldwide lack access to clean water at all** — equivalent to twice the U.S. population ([charity: water](../sources/charity-water-program.md)).

These three figures come from different sources measuring related but not identical things (safely *managed* water vs. safely managed *sources* vs. access to *clean* water at all) — treat them as three separate lenses on the same crisis, not as figures that should agree with each other.

## Who bears it

- Death rates from unsafe water exceed **50 deaths per 100,000 people** in Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia, versus **below 0.1 per 100,000** in Europe — a **greater than 1000-fold difference** ([Our World in Data](../sources/owid-water-access-data.md)).
- Women and girls handle water collection in **7-8 out of 10 households** without on-premise water access, and children under five are **over 20 times more likely** to die from unsafe water-related illness than from conflict ([charity: water](../sources/charity-water-program.md)).

## What SDG 6 asks for

Eight targets ([full list](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md)), the most relevant to this challenge:

- **6.3** — improve water quality by reducing pollution and hazardous chemical release
- **6.4** — substantially increase water-use efficiency and ensure sustainable withdrawals
- **6.b** — strengthen local community participation in water and sanitation management

Target **6.6** (protect and restore water-related ecosystems) had a **2020** deadline that has already passed — worth noting if a pitch cites SDG 6 as a live, on-track framework.

## Why this matters for region choice

Neither Los Angeles nor South Korea is the "1 in 10 with no access at all" case charity: water's model targets — see [existing-solutions.md](existing-solutions.md). Both are higher-income, connected-infrastructure settings where the problem is efficiency, drought resilience, or trust rather than basic access. That's a real scoping decision for this team: match the solution's model to the actual gap, not to the global-crisis framing.
