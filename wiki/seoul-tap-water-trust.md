# Seoul: Tap Water Trust

## The problem isn't water quality — it's trust

Seoul's tap water, Arisu, is tested for **350 different substances**, "far surpassing both national and World Health Organization requirements" ([Korea Times](../sources/korea-tap-water-trust.md)). By quality metrics, the water is not the problem. Trust is:

- **75% of Seoul citizens now drink Arisu** — a record high, up 5.4 percentage points year over year. 56.3% drink it at home; 18.7% in public spaces like parks, sports facilities, and walking trails.
- Overall satisfaction with tap water: **79.9%** (up from 77.4% in 2024). Satisfaction with water quality specifically: **82.2%** (up from 78.2%).
- More than 70% called Arisu "world-class"; nearly 80% called it a civic asset worth protecting.

(All figures: [Korea Times](../sources/korea-tap-water-trust.md).)

## Why the remaining ~25% still won't drink it

- **71% cite concerns about old residential pipes.**
- **44.5% cite lingering doubts over filtration or safety.**
- Seoul has been replacing old pipes since 2007, targeting **more than 565,000 households**, and the replacement effort is **94% complete**.
- **Water purifiers are now in about half of all Korean homes** — a behavior that predates and outlasts the water-quality problem it was a response to.

(All figures: [Korea Times](../sources/korea-tap-water-trust.md).)

## Where the distrust came from

"During Korea's breakneck industrialization in the 1970s and '80s, the country's tap water was widely regarded as unsafe, hobbled by aging pipes, insufficient filtration and unchecked industrial runoff that fouled its rivers. The result was deep public mistrust, and a national habit of boiling water before drinking it." ([Korea Times](../sources/korea-tap-water-trust.md))

This is the key structural insight: the underlying cause (bad water) was fixed, but the downstream behavior (distrust, boiling, buying purifiers) didn't automatically follow. That gap — between infrastructure quality and household behavior — is itself a "use water more wisely" problem, distinct from LA's capture problem.
