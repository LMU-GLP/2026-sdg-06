# SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation — Overview

The UN's Sustainable Development Goal 6 sets eight targets on drinking water, sanitation, water quality, water-use efficiency, integrated water management, ecosystems, and cooperation, most due by 2030. Full target text and indicators: [UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md).

## The targets that matter most for this challenge

Our team's question — "how might we help communities protect clean water and use it more wisely?" — maps most directly onto two targets:

- **Target 6.4** (water-use efficiency and sustainable withdrawals) — the "use it more wisely" half.
- **Target 6.3** (water quality, reducing pollution) and **6.6** (protecting water-related ecosystems) — the "protect" half.

Exact wording for all eight targets, plus 6.1 (drinking water access) and 6.2 (sanitation), is in [UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md).

## Where the goal stands globally

- **2.1 billion people** lack safely managed drinking water and **3.4 billion** lack safely managed sanitation, per the UN's own tracking ([UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md)).
- A separate WHO fact sheet gives a different framing of drinking water specifically: **73% of the global population (6 billion people)** used safely managed drinking-water services in 2022, with **2.2 billion** lacking them ([WHO drinking-water fact sheet](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)).

**These two figures — "2.1 billion lack safely managed drinking water" (UN SDG page) and "2.2 billion lacked safely managed drinking-water services in 2022" (WHO)** — are close but not identical, and the sources don't specify whether they're drawn from the same underlying dataset year. Keep both; don't average or reconcile them without a source that explains the discrepancy.

- Water stress: **over 2 billion people live in water-stressed countries** as of 2021 ([WHO drinking-water fact sheet](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)). This is a different measurement (water stress, i.e., withdrawal relative to available freshwater) from the access figures above — see Rule 5 in this wiki's charter and don't conflate them.
