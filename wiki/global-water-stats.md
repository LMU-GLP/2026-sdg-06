# Global Water Access: The Numbers

Two figures anchor how bad the global gap in safe water still is, both from the WHO drinking-water fact sheet ([source](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)):

- **73% of the global population (6 billion people)** used safely managed drinking-water services in 2022.
- **2.2 billion people** lacked safely managed drinking-water services in 2022 — of these, **1.5 billion** had only basic service (improved source, but over a 30-minute round trip or worse falls outside "basic"), **292 million** had limited service (over 30-minute round trip), **296 million** used unprotected wells or springs, and **115 million** collected untreated surface water directly.

## Health cost of unsafe water

- **At least 1.7 billion people** (2022) used a drinking-water source contaminated with fecal matter ([WHO](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)).
- **~505,000 diarrheal deaths annually** are attributed specifically to microbiologically contaminated drinking water; **~1 million deaths yearly** from diarrhea caused by unsafe water, sanitation, and hygiene combined ([WHO](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)).
- **395,000 child deaths annually** (under age 5) are considered preventable by addressing water, sanitation, and hygiene risks ([WHO](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)).

## Water stress is a different measurement

- **Over 2 billion people live in water-stressed countries** as of 2021 ([WHO](../sources/who-drinking-water-factsheet.md)).

This is not an access figure — it measures freshwater withdrawal against available freshwater resources (SDG indicator 6.4.2, see [UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md)), not whether a household's water source is safely managed. A country can have low water stress and still have low safe-access rates, or vice versa. Don't combine this number with the access figures above.

## Also see the UN's own version of these numbers

[UN SDG 6 targets](../sources/un-sdg6-targets.md) cites **2.1 billion people** lacking safely managed drinking water — close to but not the same as WHO's 2.2 billion. Both are kept in [SDG 6 Overview](sdg6-overview.md) rather than reconciled, since neither source explains the gap.
